Seller Rescue Kit
Health Recovery Plan of Action Your data

Get a seller account back to healthy, with dates and a plan.

Amazon judges sellers on rolling windows: 60 days for order defects, 30 for late shipments, 7 for cancellations. Old problems drop off one day at a time. This kit shows the exact day each one leaves, when you'll be back under the limit, and helps you write a Plan of Action that names a real root cause.
