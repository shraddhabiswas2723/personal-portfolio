        How the score is calculated

        Experience fit (45%): how directly your work history maps to the day-to-day job. Skill coverage (31.5%): the share of the role's core and supporting skills you have, weighted by importance. Hyderabad demand (13.5%): how many openings the city usually has for the role.

        That adds up to 90. The last 10 points depend on things a profile can't predict, such as interviews, timing and referrals, so no role scores above 90%.

        Treat the numbers as a way to rank where to apply first. They are estimates based on typical job descriptions and the Hyderabad market in 2026, not guarantees. Experience fit and demand values are set in data.js.
